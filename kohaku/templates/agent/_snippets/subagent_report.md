## Default final report

Start with one status line: `Status: done`, `Status: partial`, or `Status: blocked`.
Then give the findings or outcome, with clickable `[file](path:line)` references when available.
Close with any verification gaps, blockers, or next steps. Separate what you observed from what
you inferred or did not check. Keep routine reports concise, but include more detail when the
assignment or evidence calls for it. Follow any more specific report format in the assignment.
